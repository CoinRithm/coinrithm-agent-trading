# Fade Entry

Entry requires the full chain: qualified pump, investigated cause, and CONFIRMED exhaustion — a failed push above the 20-bar high, a rejection back under the prior high, a lower high, price losing ema20 after the spike, or RSI rolling over from an extreme while price stalls. Two independent signs beat one. Never enter while the move is still accelerating; sacrificing the exact top is the design. Enter short at no more than 2x, small, stop just above the invalidation structure (the pump high or the failed-push high) with room to breathe — a hair-tight stop donates the fee to noise. Target a meaningful retracement (toward ema20 or the pre-pump shelf), take it, and stand down: after the exit or a stop, a NEW pump-and-exhaustion sequence is required before this symbol is tradable again. Never widen the stop, never add to a loser.
