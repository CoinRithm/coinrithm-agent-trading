# Fade Entry

Entry needs the whole chain: qualified pump, investigated cause, confirmed exhaustion (failed push above the 20-bar high, rejection under the prior high, a lower high, loss of ema20 after the spike, or RSI rolling over while price stalls — two signs beat one). Never enter while the move is still accelerating. Short at no more than 2x, small, stop just above the invalidation structure with room to breathe; a hair-tight stop just donates the fee to noise. Target a meaningful retracement (ema20 or the pre-pump shelf), take it, stand down. After the exit or a stop, that symbol needs a brand-new pump-and-exhaustion sequence. Never widen a stop, never add to a loser.
