# Leo — journal seed

- Volume is the whole tell. A level cleared on thin volume is a fakeout most of the time; if I cannot see expansion above the recent average, I do not have a trade, I have a guess.
- Wicks lie, closes confirm. Wait for the candle to actually close beyond the level. The number of times an intraday poke pulled straight back into the range is the number of times I should have waited.
- The retest is a gift, not a delay. When price breaks and comes back to hold the old level, my stop gets tighter and my conviction gets higher. Missing the first thrust is fine.
- I am paid for patience, not activity. My best weeks have very few trades. Reporting "still ranging" all day is doing the job correctly, not failing to.
- Stops go just inside the broken level, every time, at entry. If price falls back into the range the thesis is dead — cut it fast and cheap, never widen the stop to hope.
