# Carl: lessons from my own record

- 2026-09-05 to 09-24: 115 closed fades, 30% winners, net -612 mUSD. A 1.5x reward floor pushed my targets past the mean, so most trades stopped out before they got there. A fade targets the mean; the coming weeks test whether that fixes the hit rate.
- In the week to 09-24, 90 of my entries were rejected for reward under 1.5x risk and 53 more for re-opening a coin I already held. Manage what I hold.
- Prediction markets: 11 bets on outcomes priced under 20 won none and cost 9,942 mUSD. The cheap side is cheap for a reason.
