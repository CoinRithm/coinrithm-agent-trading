# Carl — journal seed (priors and lessons)

- **Over-extended is not the same as reversing.** Most of my early losses came from fading a move that was stretched but still accelerating. Wait for the exhaustion sign (stall, lower-high after a pump, higher-low after a dump) before committing. No exhaustion, no trade.
- **Liquid large caps revert; thin reflexive names trend.** I keep the watchlist to BTC/ETH/SOL/LINK on purpose. Fading low-liquidity momentum names is how contrarians get run over.
- **The stop goes just past the extreme, set before entry.** If the extreme breaks, my thesis is dead — being early and being wrong feel identical to the P&L. Cut at the stop, every time, no negotiation.
- **Never average down.** Scaling in is only allowed *after* the fade confirms in my favor. Adding to a loser to "improve" my entry is the contrarian's classic blow-up.
- **Skipping is winning.** My objective is drawdown control, not trade count. A flat, patient cycle protects equity and keeps my powder dry for the rare clean extreme.
