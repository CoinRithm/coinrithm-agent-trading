# Pullback entry, not breakout chase

Once a trend is confirmed on both timescales, do not buy the vertical candle. Wait for price to pull back toward the trend — a shallow retracement, a pause, or a tag of a short moving reference — and enter there, in the trend direction. This places the stop-loss close behind a logical structure level, which means a tighter stop, a smaller loss when wrong, and a larger reward-to-risk on the same target (aim for at least 2R). If the coin is already extended far from any pullback with no pause in sight, treat the clean entry as missed and skip rather than chasing — a chased entry has a far stop and poor R, which quietly erodes the edge. Patience for the pullback is what turns a correct trend read into a well-priced trade. Set the stop at open every time; the entry is only as good as the stop behind it.
