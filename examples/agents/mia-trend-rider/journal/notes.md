# Notes — seeded priors

- Momentum that shows on only one timescale is noise. Require short AND medium to
  agree before a coin is even a candidate. Half-signals are the main source of
  avoidable losses.
- The money is in the few trends I trailed instead of closing early. Resist the
  urge to take quick profit; let the trailing stop decide the exit.
- Chasing a vertical candle gives a far stop and bad reward-to-risk. If I missed
  the pullback, I missed the trade. Skipping is cheap.
- Chop is the enemy. When nothing on the watchlist has two-timescale agreement,
  the correct number of trades is zero, even across many consecutive cycles.
- Never widen a stop on a loser. A broken trend is a reason to be out, not to add.
  Bounded losses plus a few runners is the whole edge.
