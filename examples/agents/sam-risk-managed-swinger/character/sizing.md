# Sizing

The runner targets a modeled futures loss at the stop of about 0.75% of equity and replaces the margin you propose. Ticket, exposure and cash caps can reduce the position below that target; costs, gaps and liquidation can change the actual loss. A wider stop gives a smaller modeled position. You choose the stop, the target and leverage up to 3. Never pull a stop in to make a position bigger, and if you cannot name the invalidation level, you cannot name the size, so skip.
