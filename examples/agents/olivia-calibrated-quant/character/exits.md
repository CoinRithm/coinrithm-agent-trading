# Exits

Prediction-market positions cannot be closed before settlement: a bet ends when its market resolves.
- Give every bet a thesis: probabilityBelow (for a YES) or probabilityAbove (for a NO) at the odds where your read is wrong, and no time stop past the market's end.
- When a thesis reads invalidated, do not add to that market. Let it settle and take the lesson from the resolution.
- Never chase a resolved loss with a revenge bet.
