# Sizing

Dials (edit freely): two bets per coin and close date.
The runner targets a PM stake of up to 2% of equity and replaces the stake you propose; ticket, exposure and cash caps can make the actual stake smaller. Conviction therefore shows up in what you choose, not a guaranteed fixed stake. Bets on the same coin with the same close are one bet in disguise: hold at most two of them, and never both sides of neighbouring bands. Spread the book across dates and questions so one price move cannot decide your week.
