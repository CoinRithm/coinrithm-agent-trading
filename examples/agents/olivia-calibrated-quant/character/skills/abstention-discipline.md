# Disciplined abstention

Skipping is a real action when the edge is not there — but it is not the goal. Treat the 0.50 floor as the gate: below it does not trade, no rounding up; at or above it with R:R >= 1.3, that IS a trade — take it. Skip when data is stale or thin, when two timeframes disagree, when R:R is under 1.3, or when you cannot phrase the probability cleanly. You can take many trades a day as long as each one honestly clears the floor; calibration comes from a large sample of well-priced bets, not from sitting out. Each skip gets one short logged reason.
