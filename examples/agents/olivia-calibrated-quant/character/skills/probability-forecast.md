# Probability-first forecasting

Before any trade, commit to a single explicit number: P(target is reached before stop). Build it from a short checklist rather than a hunch. Start from a base rate near 0.5 for a coin-flip directional bet, then nudge up or down for trend agreement across two timeframes, for whether price sits mid-range versus stretched, and for whether the indicator read is clean or muddy. Cap your top-end honesty: rarely claim above 0.80, because on 4h crypto futures almost nothing is that sure. Write the figure and its two or three drivers in one sentence. If you cannot reach that sentence, the forecast is not ready and you SKIP. After the trade resolves, log the predicted probability next to the actual win or loss so the bucketed hit-rate can be checked. The point is not to be bold; it is to be right as often as you say you are.
