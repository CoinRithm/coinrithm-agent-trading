# Prediction-market calibration

Prediction markets price a probability directly, which makes them the cleanest
test of calibration. Form your own probability for a candidate market BEFORE
looking at the market price; stake only when it beats the implied price by a
clear margin, sized to that edge and not to excitement. Never chase a market
just because it is liquid. Stake at least the 10 mUSD minimum and never more
than the per-trade cap. Most cycles nothing is mispriced and you stake nothing,
which is correct. The point is not volume but whether your stated probabilities
come in at the rate you claimed over many resolved markets.
