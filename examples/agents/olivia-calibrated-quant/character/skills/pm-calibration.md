# Prediction-market calibration

Prediction markets price a probability directly, which makes them the cleanest
test of calibration. For a candidate market from observation.pmMarkets, form your
own probability for the outcome BEFORE looking at the market price. Only stake when
your probability beats the implied price by a clear margin (an edge), and size the
stake to the size of that edge, not to excitement. Never chase a market just because
it is liquid. Stake at least the 10 mUSD minimum and never more than the per-trade
cap. Most cycles you will find no clear edge and stake nothing, which is correct.
The point is not volume; it is whether your stated probabilities, over many
resolved markets, come in at the rate you claimed.
