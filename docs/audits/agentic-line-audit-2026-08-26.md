# Agentic Line Audit: Final Report (2026-08-26)

## 1. Verdict

The product is well ahead of the business. CoinRithm is, verifiably, the only always-on bring-your-own-agent arena with public decision receipts and prediction-market coverage; the category leader (nof1) has been dormant since 2025-12-03 and no surveyed competitor ships BYO agents + verified paper records + PM support. The core plumbing is genuinely good: 38 tools live on the hosted MCP, contract byte-identical to repo, honest rate-limit docs, a sound 6-step studio funnel. But commercially the line is pre-traction, and since monetization is deliberately dark, its entire business value today is acquisition and option value, which makes the numbers stark: 70 keys ever, 32 users, 16 ever deployed an agent, roughly 4 humans active in 30 days, exactly 1 engaged external user (Tibor), 2 GitHub stars, npm decaying from a 711/wk peak to ~200-350/wk. And on the day of the NVIDIA EOL, the front door is actively wrong: the studio still offers a provider-dead model, and the FAQ JSON-LD, llms.txt, README, and flagship article all sell retired models to the #1 acquisition channel. Meanwhile the strongest differentiators (scorecard history, mechanical benchmarks, competitions, signed receipts) are built and switched off. The constraint is distribution plus truth-of-copy, not capability.

## 4. What NOT to build

- **Real-money graduation or funded-account tie-ins** (Alpaca/wickfunded envy). Paper-only is the ruling; the portable-credential framing is the counter-position, not a compromise.
- **Token or prize incentives** (Recall envy). ElizaOS was declared dead by its founder; Virtuals serves speculation. Compete on venue breadth and zero-ceremony entry, not prize economics.
- **Multi-provider hosted free tier.** One shared NIM key already 429s at 25 agents. Model diversity is BYO via self-host/MCP; keep 2-3 contrast models on house agents only and market model-agnosticism.
- **A general spot/futures backtesting engine.** Candles cap at 90 days of 4h bars and raw feeds carry zero redistribution rights. If backtesting ships, it is the PM replay-lite of G9, nothing bigger.
- **Generic webhooks/event push.** 60s cadence bounds latency for paper trading; the existing SSE stream can power a later wake-on-trigger. No webhook infra first.
- **A hosted user-code sandbox.** Self-host plus the full API is the escape hatch; expose OKF indicator parameters instead.
- **More runtime capability before activation is instrumented.** The confirmed P0 says this is a distribution problem, not a capacity problem.
- **Tool-parity re-sync or a broad directory blitz.** Parity debt was paid 08-19; Smithery is occupied. Only the three verified-missing listings are worth touching.
